3 things it does

What it is

A local CRM that lives in Chrome. Collect people from profiles, posts, or hashtags, organise them into buckets, and track every follow & DM.

How it works

It drives your visible Instagram tab like a real person — natural mouse movement, real scrolling, real clicks — with zero private API calls.

Why it works

Hard daily limits, randomized delays, and local-only storage. No servers, no sign-up, no spreadsheets — your data stays on your machine.

Install in 1 minute

  1. Download the zip and unzip it.
  2. Open chrome://extensions and switch on Developer mode.
  3. Click Load unpacked and pick the unzipped folder.
  4. Open Instagram, click the extension icon, and start collecting.
